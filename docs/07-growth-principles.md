# Growth principles

1. **Consistency beats everything.** Same time every day (8 PM). This grew Dynamo and Mortal more than any gear did.
2. **Audio before video.** Spend on the mic first. Viewers stay through 720p video, but leave over bad audio.
3. **Reels are the discovery engine.** Clip every stream. Short gaming reels are the main way new viewers in India find streamers.
4. **Cross-promote inside the squad.** Members appearing on each other's streams is the fastest subscriber exchange between the three channels.
5. **One community, not three.** A shared Discord server or WhatsApp community turns three small audiences into one real one.
6. **Never go public untested.** Unlisted test first; most first-stream disasters are audio problems a test would have caught.
7. **Wired over wireless.** Capture card over AirPlay, LAN cable over WiFi. One drop in front of viewers costs more than the cable.
