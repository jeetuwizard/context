# Team and workflow

## Structure

- Each of the three members streams on **their own YouTube channel**.
- They share **one Instagram (@jeetuwizard)** and the **Jeetu Wizard Facebook Page**, owned by Jitender.
- Teammates contribute content; in return every reel credits them and all three YouTube channels are linked in the Instagram bio (Instagram allows 5 links).

## Roles and permissions

Managed in **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com), which controls the Facebook Page and the linked Instagram together.

| Person | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Jitender | Admin / owner | Facebook access with full control. Only person who publishes. |
| Teammate 1 — _name_ | Editor | Task access: **Content** (creates drafts of posts and reels) |
| Teammate 2 — _name_ | Moderator | Task access: **Messages and community activity** (comments, DMs, hiding spam) |

- Each teammate uses **their own personal Facebook account**. No passwords are shared. Access is removed in two clicks.
- Where to add people: Page → Settings → Page setup → Page access → People with task access → Add new.

## Posting workflow

- **Instagram is the primary platform; Facebook mirrors it.**
- Jitender posts from the Instagram app with auto-share to Facebook on (Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Sharing across profiles). Instagram → Facebook cross-posting is reliable; the reverse is not.
- Teammates create drafts in the Business Suite **Planner** (posts to both FB + IG together). Jitender reviews and publishes.
- Alternative low-tech flow: teammates drop clips in a shared WhatsApp group or Drive folder; Jitender posts.

## Content rhythm

- **Before each stream:** Instagram story "LIVE in 1 hour 🔴" with a YouTube link sticker.
- **After each stream:** one highlight reel, 15–30 seconds, vertical 9:16, watermarked, crediting whoever's clip it is (e.g. "Clutch by @name — full stream link in bio").
- **Instagram launch rule:** post 3–4 reels before promoting the page anywhere, so visitors never land on an empty grid. Follow 10–20 relevant accounts (teammates, BGMI pages).

## Agreements to put in writing

One WhatsApp message all three confirm: the Instagram and Facebook Page belong to Jitender; teammates are contributors who get credit and promotion; how any future revenue from the shared pages is split; what happens if someone leaves.
