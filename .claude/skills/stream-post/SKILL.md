# Write stream and post text

## Voice

- Hinglish, energetic, friendly: "doston", "full masti", "roz aata hoon live"
- Brand: Jeetu — The BGMI Wizard 🧙‍♂️; signature word WWCD 🐔
- Emojis in moderation: 🧙‍♂️ 🔴 🐔 🎯 🔥
- Fixed links: youtube.com/@JeetuWizard, instagram.com/jeetuwizard

## YouTube live title

Under 70 characters, the hook first, then the brand. Patterns:
- `🔴 BGMI LIVE | Rush Gameplay + Custom Rooms | Jeetu Wizard`
- `🔴 Road to Conqueror Day N | BGMI Live | Jeetu Wizard`
- `🔴 5 WWCD Challenge 🐔 | BGMI Live`

## YouTube live description

Start from the channel description in `docs/04-texts.md`, then add: what today's stream is about, squad members playing (with their channel links), the Instagram link, and `#BGMI #BGMILive #JeetuWizard #WWCD`.

## Instagram reel caption

One hook line, the credit ("Clutch by @name"), a call to action ("Full stream on YouTube — link in bio 🔴"), 3–5 hashtags.

## Going-live story

Short: "LIVE in 1 hour 🔴 Aaj [plan] — aa jao!" plus a reminder to add the YouTube link sticker.

Give 2–3 options for titles, one for everything else. Keep texts for recurring series consistent with earlier ones if the user mentions them.
